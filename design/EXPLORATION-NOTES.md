# Exploration Notes

Candidate concepts below are **research ideas**, not finished results. Their placement in one domain is for discoverability, not a judgment about their fundamental nature.

## Cross-domain pilots

- **Literature** — an artistic/cultural practice and collection of works.
- **Forest** — an ecological system with interconnected processes.
- **Company** — a formal organisation embedded in institutions and markets.
- **Chinese History** — a large-scale historical field and unfolding sequence of events.

These contrast different primary natures and help test whether one inquiry method can adapt without manufacturing generic categories.

## Open design questions

- How should the atlas cross-link concepts with more than one relevant domain without duplicating content?
- How should a concept page change when there is no stable or agreed defining essence?
- When does the integrated account need more than one explanatory model?
- How can completed research be verified and revised without hiding its epistemic status?

Keep proposed methods distinct from verified facts, and leave room for further exploration.
